Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

What's Stored on Your Device

Your language preference and saved guides are stored locally on your phone. That's it — this app doesn't create an account for you, and there's nothing to sign into.

Tapaiko language preference ra saved guides matra tapaiko phone ma local ma store huncha. Yati nai ho — yo app le tapaiko lagi kunai account banaudaina, ra login garnu parne kehi chaina.

What We Don't Collect

No analytics, no ad tracking, no usage data sent anywhere. This app doesn't run its own server, so there's nowhere for that kind of data to go even if we wanted to collect it.

Kunai analytics chaina, kunai ad tracking chaina, katai pani usage data pathaidaina. Yo app le aafnai server pani chalaudaina, tesaile hami le chahe pani testo data jane thau nai chaina.

Local Notifications

If you turn on Guide Recheck Reminders, the reminder is scheduled entirely on your phone using your device's own notification system — nothing is sent to any server to set it up or trigger it.

Guide Recheck Reminders on garnu vayo vane, reminder purna roop ma tapaikai phone ma, tapaikai device ko aafnai notification system use garera schedule huncha — yeslai set up ya trigger garna kunai server ma kehi pathaidaina.

The Feedback Form

If you choose to submit feedback through the form linked on the Help & Support page, that goes to Google Forms and is handled under Google's own privacy policy — this app itself never sees or stores anything you submit there.

Help & Support page ma vayeko form marfat feedback submit garnu vayo vane, tyo Google Forms ma jancha ra Google kai privacy policy antargat handle huncha — yo app le aafai tyaha submit gareko kunai pani kura kahile dekhdaina ya store gardaina.

Changes to This Policy

If how this app handles data ever changes, this page will be updated to reflect it.

Yo app le data handle garne tarika kahile change vaye, yo page update garincha.

Questions about this policy? Contact khaling.studio@gmail.com.
